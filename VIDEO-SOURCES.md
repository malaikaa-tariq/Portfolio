# Architectural background video sources

The website uses free Pexels footage for atmospheric background motion. The footage is deliberately kept separate from Hassan Nawaz’s project gallery.

- Modern house exterior: https://www.pexels.com/video/an-exterior-design-of-a-modern-house-7578541/
- Building construction timelapse: https://www.pexels.com/video/building-construction-timelapse-19479036/
- Modern office interior: https://www.pexels.com/video/modern-office-interior-5716999/
- Modern house interior: https://www.pexels.com/video/interior-design-of-a-modern-house-3773486/
- Pexels licence: https://www.pexels.com/license/

Pexels states that its photos and videos may be used and modified for free, including on websites. Review the current Pexels licence before final commercial deployment.

For better loading performance on the final business domain, download approved 1080p versions, compress them, place them in `public/assets/videos/`, and replace the remote URLs in `src/App.jsx`.
